How it works

Workflow

Preview before mint

  1. 01
    Describe

    Give the agent a character, role, clothing direction and mood.

  2. 02
    Assign

    Census creates a secure draft and locks the immutable traits.

  3. 03
    Preview

    The agent must display the real palette-exact 40×40 PNG in the conversation.

  4. 04
    Approve

    Check readability, complete head framing and Species anatomy. Nothing is sent before approval.

  5. 05
    Mint

    The same sender simulates the exact Sepolia transaction, then submits it.
